🛡️ Our Core Commitment: Instaloop was built as a sanctuary of quietude. We do NOT track, collect, monetize, or sell your personal habits, biometric pauses, or browsing behavior. Your attention and your data belong entirely to you.

1. Information We Collect (And What We Don't)

Instaloop operates primarily on-device (100% offline).

  • No Account Required for Core Wellness: You can use the Breathing Dial, Manifestation Alarms, and Doomscroll Shield completely offline without creating an account.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sync your Sacred Bonsai streak or connect with friends, we store your chosen public handle (e.g. @username) and email address securely.
  • No Biometric Data Collected: Your breathing sessions, stillness durations, and screen pauses are calculated and stored locally on your device.

2. Accessibility Service & App Usage Declaration

To provide the Doomscroll Shield and Native Gatekeeper, Instaloop utilizes Android's AccessibilityService and/or UsageStatsManager API.

  • Purpose: The Accessibility Service is used strictly to detect when an addictive application designated by you (such as Instagram or YouTube) enters the foreground, allowing Instaloop to display a gentle 15-second mindful breath pause before you enter an infinite scroll loop.
  • Strict Data Boundaries: The Accessibility Service is NOT used to read personal messages, capture keystrokes, record audio/video, track search queries, or inspect private content.
  • Zero Transmission: All app-detection logic happens locally on your device's processor. No application usage records are ever transmitted to our servers or third parties.

3. Mindful Friends & Present Moment Queue

When you exchange mindful micro-pauses with friends:

  • Messages wait peacefully in an asynchronous Present Moment Queue and automatically expire after 15 days.
  • We do not scan your contacts or send unsolicited invites. Connection occurs solely through explicit user sharing.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Trackers

Instaloop contains ZERO third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or behavioral tracking trackers. We do not participate in advertising networks or cross-app profiling.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have sovereign control over your digital footprint:

  • You can reset your local streaks, clear your shield statistics, or delete your account at any time directly within the app settings.
  • Upon account deletion, all associated handle records and unread pauses are immediately purged from our servers.
